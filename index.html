Output falls about 3.5% per 1,000 ft of elevation and 1% per 10°F above 60°F. Elevation is the one that matters — sea level to 8,000 ft is a 28% swing, while the entire national range of summer design temperatures spans about 3%.

Cooling & heating

The AC compressor is usually what sizes the generator. Enter tonnage (typical LRA auto-fills) or nameplate LRA if you have it. Heating and cooling are non-coincident — the larger of the two counts.

Well & Pumps

Motor loads with real inrush. Check what the house has; adjust HP if known.

Kitchen, laundry & general loads

Nameplate watts. These go into the NEC demand-factor pool (first 8 kW at 100%, remainder at 40%). E = include in Essentials option. M = on a load-management module (sheds/staggers).

Other Loads

Anything else — hot tub, shop equipment, medical equipment. Add running watts (and starting watts for motors).
